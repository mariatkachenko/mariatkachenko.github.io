# MTS Pay Vanishing Flyout Design

When MTS Pay becomes the centered `/works` card, its logo and butterfly launch toward the viewer in sequence. The logo reaches roughly 3.2× scale and the butterfly roughly 3.8×. Both stay sharp and visible through the readable first half, then progressively blur and fade during the final approach until fully invisible. The butterfly begins 90ms after the logo. Returning to the card restarts the choreography. Mobile uses smaller terminal scales and blur; reduced-motion shows neither animated flyout.
